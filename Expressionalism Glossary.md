# Expressionalism Glossary

This glossary gives short working definitions of the terms used in the chapters of Volumes Two and Three: the Framework, the Unified Formal Model and Computational Substrate, the Toolkit, the Critiques and Objections, Metrics Checks and the Acknowledgements, all part of Expressionalism: A Schema of Relational Obtainability and Pointing. Each definition follows how the term is used in those chapters, quoting or closely paraphrasing them, and points to where it is used; the chapter text governs where the two differ. Like everything in the schema, the definitions are provisional and open to re-expression under P8.

Entries are alphabetical. Pointers name a chapter and a presumption, phase or section, for example (Framework P3), (Toolkit, Phase 5), (Unified Formal Model §6.2) or (Metrics Checks §7). Formulas appear in the entries that use them and are gathered in the Symbols and Formulas table at the end.

## A

**Abduction** (*Toolkit, Stage 1*). To abduct is to draw provisional candidates (secondary expressions, proto-claims, or a subset of the starting points) for the current primary expression. In a run, selecting and populating the secondary expressions is called Secondary Abduction. *See also:* Secondary Abduction; Peircean Abduction Heuristic; Proto-claims and secondary claims.

**Acknowledgements** (*Volume Three*). The philosophical traditions, concepts and formal resources that supplied generative starting points for the framework, each treated as a provisional secondary expression. Where the framework departs from a source, the entry says so. *See also:* Dialectical; Framework, The.

**Alien Mode** (*Toolkit, Mode Control Toggles*). A toggle, default Off. When on, it forces granular raw bytes, bits or pixels, widens sigma by +0.15, boosts uncertainty automatically if resonance is low, and sets Density Probe On and Guardrails Off; it counters interpretive creep for pure internal or alien inputs. *See also:* Mode Control Toggles; Sigma (σ).

**Alignability (AlignSpectrum)** (*Framework P6; Unified Formal Model §8*). Comparability as a spectrum of alignability and transduction (sparse, partial, or dense) that provisions distances and divergences as utilities. In the Unified Formal Model, AlignSpectrum ∈ [0, 1] models the spectrum of transduction and comparison between secondary expressions and the primary expression. *See also:* P6: Comparability and Measurement of Expressions; Secondary Transduction Gate; Transduction.

**Alignment to Primary** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Toolkit, Metrics*). The relational connectivity of a surviving secondary to the primary, with the licensed default Alignmentᵢ = 1 − JSD(Pᵢ ‖ P_primary) on a shared feature space. Its mean carries weight 0.40 in the Secondary Coherence Score, and it appears as Align in the Secondary Synthesis Ledger. *See also:* Secondary Coherence Score (SCS); Soft distribution; Primary-as-Anchor Priority.

**All-isolated edge case** (*Toolkit, Phase 6*). The state in which every component has traction ≤ τ: ρ = 1, the Secondary Coherence Score shows “N/A (all components isolated)”, the yields are undefined, and the Yield Type is forced to Raw/Regressed. The full template still prints, with both Phase 5 ledgers stating that no items were generated. This is the terminal state the Staging Axiom registers; it is not the no-input halt. *See also:* Staging Axiom; No-input halt; Raw/Regressed Yield.

**All Mode** (*Toolkit, Stage 1*). The balanced-defaults choice that opens Stage 1, beside Customize, which allows exact overrides of any toggle or sub-mode. Every toggle starts at its listed default, and the input-triggered rules (for example Non-Dual for void or paradox, Contrarian for high ambiguity or low input variance, Pentalemma Variant for non-binary flows, Phenomenal Probe for phenomena) still fire unless the user has set that toggle; any toggle switched on automatically is listed under Toggles Active in the Header. Its baseline is rich output, with Show Full Density Metrics, Density Probe, Secondary Synthesis Audit and Uncertainty Probe On. *See also:* Toggles Active; Toggle groups; Header.

**Atomic expression** (*Framework P4*). A sequence of relateds that functions as a relator while standing as a related when measured alone; the middle of the three layers (subatomic singles, atomic sequences, complex nests). *See also:* Subatomic expression; Complex expression; Layer Spectrum (LayerSpectrum).

**Avg Density** (*Toolkit, Phase 1 and Supporting Diagnostics*). A Phase 1 yield of the Density Probe, read on the same Phase-1 distribution that Computational Substrate §4.6 uses for Tsallis density; the Toolkit gives ~0.5 as its middle reading. It is a named readout with no formula in this book. *See also:* Tsallis density (ϕ); Layer Spectrum Avg; Supporting Diagnostics.

## B

**Balanced Yield** (*Framework P7*). The yield type with high certainty dominance, one of the four types on the yield spectrum. *See also:* Yield Type; Tensive Yield; Tempered Yield; Raw/Regressed Yield.

**Bifurcation** (*Framework P3; Toolkit P3*). The split of contrasting parts into connectable paths, available for further synthesis, or unconnectable gaps, which register as limits of synthesis. *See also:* Relational construction; Non-relational gaps.

**Bootstrap / origination asymmetry** (*Computational Substrate §2 and §7; Unified Formal Model, opening*). The formal apparatus is defined only for inputs already presented as primary expressions and never claims to generate the first expression or the first secondary. The asymmetry cannot be removed without unexamined posits that would violate P1, so the Staging Axiom registers it as a permanent, labeled operational limit; no prior field is installed and no ex-nihilo generation is claimed. *See also:* Staging Axiom; Primitive.

## C

**Canonical Output Template** (*Toolkit*). The layout every run follows, in this order (the Fixed Structure): Header; Run Metadata; Executive Yield (Diagnostics Block); Inferred Relator Mode Distribution; Hard Isolation Summary; Contrast & Non-Relational Gap Summary + Audit Summary; Visual Diagnostics (Secondary Synthesis Ledger + Traction Flow Diagram); Uncertainty Table; Tempered Certainty Ledger; Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger; Metric Evolution Table; Supporting Diagnostics; The Four Takes; P8 Gate; Optional Next Steps; Closing statement. Its Canonical Output Skeleton shows the required section order and table shapes; every slot is mandatory, and a blank means unfilled, not optional. The stated exceptions are the no-input halt note and Light Output. *See also:* Locks; Light Output; No-input halt.

**Certainties** (*Framework, Introduction; Toolkit, Phase 3*). The connectable side of the tensive ratio: the contrasts, alignments and relational utilities that hold. Certainty is assessed by the degree to which secondary expressions align with one another and point back to the primary expression; in a run, Phase 3 builds certainties from high-alignment survivors (minimum 5, cap 10). *See also:* Uncertainties; Meaning; Certainty Table.

**Certainty Table** (*Toolkit, Phase 3*). The table of certainties built from high-alignment survivors. Components at or below τ are moved to the Uncertainty Table and excluded from it; its Inter-Coherence values are the per-secondary values whose mean is the Inter-Secondary Coherence term of the Secondary Coherence Score. *See also:* Certainties; Tempered Certainty Ledger; Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC).

**Certainty Temper Index** (*Toolkit, Phase 5 and Supporting Diagnostics*). A Phase 5 readout shown in the Temper Index column of the Tempered Certainty Ledger and in Supporting Diagnostics. It is a named readout with no formula in this book. *See also:* Tempered Certainty Ledger; Reflexive Tension.

**Certainty Temper Probe** (*Toolkit, Certainty Tempering toggles*). A toggle, default On (Deep), that runs deep certainty tempering (layered recursion); its Light sub-mode gives surface-level doubts. Under Deep, every retained secondary receives a full row in the Tempered Certainty Ledger. *See also:* Temper-Probe; Tempered Certainty Ledger.

**Certainty yield geo avg** (*Toolkit, Phase 3*). The geometric average of the certainty yields in Phase 3, with a stated target of 0.7–0.9. *See also:* Certainties; uncertainty_yield_geo_avg.

**Chaos Scale** (*Toolkit, Specialized Probes*). A parameter, default 0.05; higher values increase porosity and chaotic equity (wider sigma). The chaos adjustment also enters Uncertainty Stability. *See also:* Sigma (σ); Uncertainty Stability.

**Claim Coherence** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). The average alignment of the abducted secondary claims, tempered by uncertainty_prob_global_avg. *See also:* Proto-claims and secondary claims; Secondary Claim Temper (SCT).

**Claim-Tempered Yield** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Toolkit, Yields*). The Tensive yield adjusted by Secondary Claim Temper: Claim-Tempered = Tensive × (1 − 0.1 × SCT). With SCT clamped to [0.3, 0.7] the factor stays between 0.93 and 0.97, so the tempering is small (Metrics Checks §7). A run shows the figures entered. *See also:* Tensive; Secondary Claim Temper (SCT).

**Complex expression** (*Framework P4*). A nest containing multiple relateds and at least one relator, with unlimited nesting depth permitted. *See also:* Atomic expression; Subatomic expression; Layer Spectrum (LayerSpectrum).

**Computational Substrate** (*Volume Two*). The chapter that gives the reasons for each parameter of the Unified Formal Model and the Toolkit against the seven design criteria, and maps the Toolkit as an operational specialization of the general apparatus. Under its Governance Protocol every parameter stays revisable. *See also:* Seven Design Criteria; Governance Protocol; Unified Formal Model.

**Connectability gradient** (*Framework P3; Critiques 3*). One of the distinctions of relational construction: which differences can be carried forward relationally and which cannot. With the repeatability spectrum and the two primary modes, it makes up the relational density spectrum of P3. *See also:* Relational Density Spectrum; Repeatability Spectrum.

**Contrarian** (*Toolkit, Uncertainty toggles*). A toggle, default Off; when on, it skews input variance by −0.1 and adds gap probing, and its Off state is labeled “Neutral”. It teases out voids and orthogonals and switches on automatically for high ambiguity or low input variance. *See also:* All Mode; Input variance.

**Contrasting parts** (*Framework P2*). The differentiations that appear when secondary expressions are brought into relation with one another, once analysis proceeds under P1’s Door 1. If the secondary expressions stay uniform, the pointing-aspect stays minimally differentiated and the expression registers as minimally usable for further analysis. *See also:* P2: Contrasting Parts in the Field; Bifurcation; P1 Door 1 and Door 2.

**Critiques and Objections** (*Volume Two*). Twenty-one objections to the framework, grouped by kind, each with a response. The responses draw on P1–P8 and the Toolkit’s mechanics, and several leave the judgment with the reader at the P8 Gate. *See also:* Look; P8 Gate.

**Current primary expression** (*Framework P5*). The immediate totality under analysis, treated primarily as a related at ~1 certainty and co-existing with provisional secondary expressions. It stays dynamic during a run, while the secondaries are held static. *See also:* Primary expression; Primary-as-Anchor Priority.

## D

**Data Type** (*Toolkit, Header*). The Header field that states how the run read the input: linguistic, non-linguistic or phenomenal. The medium does not settle this; taking an input one way is itself a secondary, so the runner decides unless the user specifies. Inputs read as phenomenal or non-linguistic take data_type = ‘phenomenon’, with sigma widened to 0.12. *See also:* Header; Phenomenal Probe; Sigma (σ).

**Decomposition Gate** (*Toolkit P3*). The Toolkit’s short name for its P3 gate (Bifurcation, Repeatability, and Decomposition Gate), where connectable paths decompose into relateds (passive anchors) and relators (active connectors in self-pointing or other-pointing mode). *See also:* Gates; Related; Relator.

**Default interpretive restraint** (*Framework P6*). When traction is low or Synthesis Refusal appears, further interpretive gestures stay relatively weak unless the secondary expressions at hand give strong grounds to override. The restraint is an operational heuristic and fully overridable; any change to it can itself be treated as a new expression under P8. *See also:* Synthesis Refusal; Non-relational gaps.

**Density Probe** (*Toolkit, Mode Control Toggles*). A toggle, default On (Base), that computes internal variances and layering metrics, with sub-modes Base, Fuzzy, Weighted and Iterative. Its partition (the relational-density dimensions of P3) is the primary licensed default feature space for soft distributions. *See also:* Avg Density; Layer Spectrum Avg; Soft distribution.

**Dialectical** (*Framework, Introduction and P7; Acknowledgements, Hegel*). The certainty–uncertainty aggregation of P7, which adjudicates the dialectical sum into a provisional yield; Stage 2 of the Toolkit is Dialectical and Secondary Expression Analysis. It is taken from Hegel stripped of teleological necessity and of any claim to final synthesis. *See also:* P7: Evaluation and Yield; Acknowledgements; Yield Spectrum.

**Dilemma gate** (*Framework P1; Unified Formal Model §4*). The operational threshold within analysis that separates relational construction from generative uncertainty or halt; it is the comparison of the hybrid score H(x) with τ. Above τ the analysis proceeds (P1 Door 1); at or below τ it registers generative uncertainty or silence (P1 Door 2), and a component at or below τ triggers hard isolation. *See also:* Tau (τ); P1 Door 1 and Door 2; Hard Isolation Rule.

**Directedness differential (δ)** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Computational Substrate §4.7; Toolkit, Metrics*). A supporting reading of the directed asymmetry between a surviving secondary and the primary, on the same soft distributions as Alignment to Primary: δᵢ = clamp(D_i→p / (D_i→p + D_p→i + ε), 0, 1), ε ≥ 10⁻¹², where D_i→p = KL(Pᵢ ‖ P_primary) and D_p→i = KL(P_primary ‖ Pᵢ). It enters no isolation decision, core yield, mode distribution or SCS by default (weight 0; any positive weight is a declared P8 choice). For an isolated component it is undefined and labeled “pointing refused”. *See also:* Pointing refused; Supporting Diagnostics.

**Directional Bleed** (*Toolkit, Equity and Secondary Sourcing toggles*). A sub-toggle, default Off: bidirectional resonance, with +0.1 tension if asymmetry exceeds 0.2; Off is normal unidirectional. *See also:* Secondary Expression Equity.

**Dirichlet priors** (*Toolkit, Stage 1 and Phase 2*). The priors used in Secondary Abduction for equity-balanced sampling of candidate secondaries across domains and traditions. *See also:* Secondary Abduction; Equity Sampler.

**Doors** (*Framework P1 and P8*). There are two pairs, and they are distinct: the Doors of P1 (proceed, or generative uncertainty or silence) and the Doors of P8 (provisional acceptance, or open re-expression). *See also:* P1 Door 1 and Door 2; P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2.

## E

**Eight presumptions (P1–P8)** (*Framework; Toolkit*). The eight if-then presumptions of the Framework, from P1 (The Open Field) to P8 (Perception and Orientation), each articulating functional distinctions that emerge within analysis. The Toolkit calls them starting points and abducts subsets of three to eight of them in each run. *See also:* P1: The Open Field; P8: Perception and Orientation; Unaddressed Starting Point.

**Emptiness-First (Non-Dual Priority)** (*Toolkit, Uncertainty toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that reverses relational bias: it treats gaps as the primary lens and high-uncertainty yields as virtuous. Isolated mass may be foregrounded interpretively as the expression’s non-relational gaps; this is an overlay only, and ρ is not altered. *See also:* Non-Dual; Isolation Residual (ρ).

**Equity Sampler** (*Toolkit, Equity and Secondary Sourcing toggles*). A sub-toggle, default Off, that perturbs noise from the Dirichlet priors, for equity toward marginals and chaos emulation. *See also:* Dirichlet priors; Secondary Expression Equity.

**Executive Yield (Diagnostics Block)** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template*). The third slot of the template, where the decisive numerics appear first: Yield Type, the Secondary Coherence Score with its three component means and the arithmetic, Tensive, and the Claim-Tempered variant with its figures entered. *See also:* Canonical Output Template; Secondary Coherence Score (SCS).

**Expression** (*Framework*). Any provisional stabilization of secondary synthesis: a thought, a claim, a work of art, a scientific assertion, a memory, a perceptual scene, a political document, or a moment of silence. The framework takes expression as its fundamental primitive; pointing is its directed aspect. *See also:* Primitive; Pointing / pointing-aspect; Secondary synthesis.

**Expression Network** (*Toolkit, Core Mechanics*). The whole model (primary node, secondary nodes, Alignment edges, Secondary Alignment edges and Traction) treated as a directed relational graph. *See also:* Traction Flow Diagram; Alignment to Primary.

## F

**Falsifiable / testable** (*Framework, A Note on Words*). A look can fail in use, through self-refutation, incoherence, or uncertainty too high to serve the purpose at hand. A high-uncertainty yield is still a legitimate yield; it fails only as support for that use, and a failed look is not a refuted framework. *See also:* Look; Falsify Mode; Incoherence check.

**Falsify Mode** (*Toolkit, Output toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that tightens or loosens the incoherence thresholds (Lenient or Strict); its Off state uses “Neutral thresholds”. A look can fail in use with it Off. *See also:* Incoherence check; Falsifiable / testable.

**Four Takes, The** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template*). The four plain-language narratives of a run: Certainty Take, Uncertainty Take, Reflexive Take and Recap Take. Each refers to the Secondary Coherence Score as a major diagnostic among survivors and names the residual-side diagnostics when isolation or post-isolation uncertainty is in play. *See also:* Residual-side diagnostics; Light Output.

**Framework, The** (*Volume Two*). The descriptive philosophical account of how an expression obtains through secondary synthesis and how its pointing-aspect can be analyzed, set out as eight presumptions. It is non-dogmatic about its own status and treats itself as one optional set of distinctions. *See also:* Eight presumptions (P1–P8); Unified Formal Model; Toolkit, The.

**Functional Role Shift** (*Framework P4 and P8; Unified Formal Model §6.7*). The same entity may function as a related at one scale and a relator at another, and under P8 these roles may flip. While hard isolation is active, role shifts are recorded as metadata and do not override it. *See also:* Related; Relator; P8: Perception and Orientation.

**Fuzzy membership function μ(x)** (*Unified Formal Model §2*). The first factor of the hybrid function. The default is the linear ramp: μ(x) = 0 if x ≤ τ; μ(x) = max(0, min(1, (x − τ)/(1 − τ))) if τ &lt; x &lt; 1; μ(x) = 1 if x = 1. The alternative is the clamped sigmoid μ(x) = clamp(1/(1 + e^(−k(x − m))), 0, 1), with defaults k = 12 and m = 0.5. *See also:* Hybrid function H(x); Normalized Bayesian posterior P_norm(x | V).

## G

**Gap** (*Framework, A Note on Words*). Anything a run can name, including a non-connection. A gap is not the Non-relational Remainder, which names only the limit and is never produced by a run (template lock: gap ≠ Non-relational Remainder). *See also:* Non-relational gaps; Non-relational Remainder; Locks.

**Gates** (*Toolkit, Summary of Core Starting Points*). Each presumption except P2 carries a named gate: Dilemma Gate (P1), Bifurcation, Repeatability, and Decomposition Gate (P3), Hierarchy Gate (P4), Scale Gate (P5), Secondary Transduction Gate (P6), Adjudication Gate (P7), and Re-Expression and Open Integration Gate (P8). In a run’s output the P8 gate is the P8 Gate slot; the Unified Formal Model calls it the Perception Gate. *See also:* Dilemma gate; P8 Gate.

**Generative pause** (*Framework, Introduction and P1*). What persistent unmappables or high-variance inputs may register as: a pause that widens the synthesis space without forcing resolution. Generative pauses sit with uncertainties and non-relational gaps on the uncertainty side of meaning’s tensive ratio, and they stay outside ρ. *See also:* Meaning; Residual pole; P1 Door 1 and Door 2.

**Governance Protocol** (*Computational Substrate §6*). Any change to a numerical or structural parameter can itself be treated as a new primary expression subject to the full P1–P8 schema, open to secondary synthesis audit, Isolation Residual registration, and renewal under P8. All coefficients, thresholds, soft-vector constructions and optional weights stay revisable under it. *See also:* Seven Design Criteria; P8: Perception and Orientation.

**Guardrails** (*Toolkit, Certainty Tempering toggles*). A toggle, default Off. When on, it enables self-refutations on incoherence or low metrics; when off, it uses resilient defaults that treat lows as tensive features. It concerns self-refutation, not ethics (Critiques 9). *See also:* Falsifiable / testable; Alien Mode.

## H

**Hard Isolation Rule** (*Unified Formal Model §§6.2–6.7; Toolkit*). A post-processing filter, non-negotiable while active: any component with traction ≤ τ is excluded from every average and distribution, moved to the Uncertainty Table with the Synthesis Refusal label, and registers only through ρ (in Tensive and in the uncertainty boost) and the reported count. Under P8, τ may be adjusted within [0.0001, 0.01], or hard isolation temporarily suspended, with visible warning and metadata logging. *See also:* Synthesis Refusal; Isolation Residual (ρ); Survivors.

**Header** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template*). The first slot of a run’s output: Primary Expression, Data Type and Toggles Active. *See also:* Data Type; Toggles Active.

**Hybrid function H(x)** (*Unified Formal Model §1*). The general form behind every primary modeling step: H(x) = min(1, μ(x) × P_norm(x | V)) ∈ [0, 1], where V is input variance, contrast, repeatability or other conditioning information in the current analysis. Its comparison with τ is the dilemma gate, and the secondary-level value Hᵢ is Traction Provided. *See also:* Fuzzy membership function μ(x); Normalized Bayesian posterior P_norm(x | V); Dilemma gate.

## I

**Incoherence check** (*Toolkit, Phases 1 and 3; Critiques 2*). A data-type-aware check that fires on std > 0.30 in Phase 1 and Phase 3. Persistent mismatch or incoherence triggers explicit flags and partial yields with boosted uncertainties; Falsify Mode tightens or loosens the thresholds. *See also:* Falsify Mode; Falsifiable / testable.

**Inferred Relator Mode Distribution** (*Unified Formal Model §6.3; Toolkit, Metrics*). The two-mode distribution, P(Self-pointing) and P(Other-pointing), computed by softmax (or an equivalent normalization) on survivors only at the end of a run; the pair sums to 1. Isolation Residual sits outside it with no probability mass and is not 1 − [P(Self-pointing) + P(Other-pointing)]. The probabilities are interpretive and the roles context-dependent. *See also:* Two primary modes; Isolation Residual (ρ).

**Inheritance and Specialization Rule** (*Unified Formal Model §10*). Every presumption inherits the hybrid function, fuzzy membership families, Bayesian normalization, dilemma gate, tetralemma mapping and hard isolation rule; specialization occurs only through the spectrum being modeled, abductively chosen parameters, and constraints or boosting rules particular to that presumption. The hard isolation rule and the Staging Axiom apply uniformly. *See also:* Unified Formal Model; Seven Design Criteria; Staging Axiom.

**Input variance** (*Toolkit, Input Scan*). The input transduced into a variance: chunks are formed, standard deviation is computed per chunk on fuzzy memberships drawn from the P1–P4 spectra, and the result is aggregated by harmonic mean clamped to [0.03, 0.6]. Low input variance produces an early uncertainty_prob_global boost (+0.15 for sparse sensations). Input variance is reported with sigma in Run Metadata. *See also:* Sigma (σ); Transduction; uncertainty_prob_global.

**Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC)** (*Computational Substrate §4.1.1; Toolkit, Metrics*). The meshing of a surviving secondary with the other survivors: the mean over j ≠ i of 1 − JSD(Pᵢ ‖ Pⱼ), where N is the number of survivors, and 1 when N = 1. Its mean carries weight 0.35 in the Secondary Coherence Score and is the Inter-Coherence (ISC) column of the ledgers. *See also:* Inter-Secondary Divergence (JSD); Secondary Coherence Score (SCS); Soft distribution.

**Inter-Secondary Divergence (JSD)** (*Computational Substrate §4.5*). The average pairwise Jensen-Shannon divergence (base 2, range [0, 1]) among the survivors’ soft distributions. Raw JSD is a supporting clash diagnostic that enters neither the mode distribution nor the SCS weights; 1 − JSD_avg is the Inter-Secondary Coherence term. *See also:* Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC).

**Isolation Residual (ρ)** (*Unified Formal Model §6.1; Toolkit; Metrics Checks §2*). The isolation density, also called the density of Synthesis Refusal: ρ = (isolated count)/N, where N counts all components, isolated and surviving: the proportion of components with traction ≤ τ. It is not a mode, receives no probability mass, is not 1 − [P(Self-pointing) + P(Other-pointing)], and is not the Non-relational Remainder. It enters Tensive through (1 − ρ) and reported uncertainty through the uncertainty boost. *See also:* Hard Isolation Rule; Residual pole; Uncertainty boost.

**Isolation Residual trigger** (*Unified Formal Model §6.1; Toolkit P3*). The flag raised when traction ≤ τ, which triggers hard isolation. It is not a third relational mode. *See also:* Hard Isolation Rule; Tau (τ).

## K

**KL (Kullback–Leibler divergence)** (*Computational Substrate §4.7; Toolkit, Metrics*). The directed divergence KL(P ‖ Q) used for the two terms of the directedness differential: D_i→p = KL(Pᵢ ‖ P_primary) and D_p→i = KL(P_primary ‖ Pᵢ), with additive smoothing if necessary. *See also:* Directedness differential (δ); Inter-Secondary Divergence (JSD).

## L

**Layer Spectrum Avg** (*Toolkit, Phase 1 and Supporting Diagnostics*). A Phase 1 yield with the stated range [0.2–0.8]. It is a named readout with no formula in this book. *See also:* Layer Spectrum (LayerSpectrum); Avg Density.

**Layer Spectrum (LayerSpectrum)** (*Framework P4; Unified Formal Model §7*). LayerSpectrum ∈ [0, 1] inherits from the relational density of P3 and models subatomic organization at low values, atomic sequences at mid values, and complex nests at high values. *See also:* Atomic expression; Layer Spectrum Avg; P4: Layering in Expressions.

**Light Output** (*Toolkit, Output toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that collapses all tables into a single flowing narrative while preserving every yield, SCS and key tension, including every tempered value and claim-stability score. It is one of two stated exceptions to the full template; the other is the no-input halt note. *See also:* Canonical Output Template; Four Takes, The.

**Locks** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Skeleton*). Label rules that hold whatever the layout: gap ≠ Non-relational Remainder; ρ = isolation density only; SCS on survivors; no invented scores; and, at the P8 Gate, yield ≠ meaning, with P8 re-expression still open. Layout may vary under P8 if a primary needs it, provided the labels and locks hold. *See also:* Canonical Output Template; P8 Gate.

**Look** (*Framework, A Note on Words; Critiques 4 and 21*). A single analysis of an expression. What the framework describes is a look, not a claim about the world (“this look is not the world”, Critiques 4); a look can serve its use or fail in use, and P8 lets it be put down (Critiques 21). *See also:* Score; Falsifiable / testable; P8 Gate.

## M

**Meaning** (*Framework*). Meaning appears as the tensive ratio between the connectable certainties an expression establishes and the uncertainties, generative pauses, and non-relational gaps it leaves behind. No figure is meaning: the Secondary Coherence Score and Tensive are a diagnostic and a yield, and Meaning Tension is a named slot left empty: no formula, no figure. *See also:* Certainties; Uncertainties; Tensive; Truth.

**Meaning-Emphasis / Truth-Emphasis** (*Toolkit, Uncertainty toggles*). A toggle pair, default Off, with auto-activation rules, that elevates the respective ratios or strengths; the Off state is labeled “Neutral”. *See also:* Meaning; Truth.

**Meaning Tension** (*Toolkit, Yields and Supporting Diagnostics*). A named dark slot, with no formula and no figure in this book’s runs. It is not meaning. *See also:* Meaning; Supporting Diagnostics.

**Meta-Loop** (*Toolkit, Output toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that re-runs up to three iterations with sub-modes Rerun-Secondaries, Rephrase-Uncertainties, Max-Uncertainties, Max-Certainties, Contrast-Runs and Doubt-Rerun. Paradox or loop hints switch it on when the user has not set it. *See also:* All Mode; Multiplicity Spectrum.

**Metric Evolution Table** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template; Unified Formal Model §6.6*). Metrics as rows (mean Align, mean ISC, mean Tr, SCS, ρ, uncertainty_prob_global, Tensive, SCT, Claim-Tempered) and phases as columns (Ph 1 to Ph 6, Final, Δ). An empty phase reads N/A, and final-only output is non-compliant. A phase with hard isolation carries the annotation “Hard isolation applied — X components excluded as Synthesis Refusal (proportion Y%).” *See also:* Stages and Phases; Hard Isolation Rule.

**Metrics Checks** (*Volume Three*). Numerical consistency checks of the locked core equations (isolation trigger, ρ, SCS on survivors, uncertainty boost, two-mode distribution, Tensive, SCT and Claim-Tempered, the all-isolated case, δ), a 10% and 20% parameter sweep, and Tsallis ϕ, on constructed and random inputs. They show that the rules behave as written on the inputs tried; they do not show that the formulas are correct, that the parameter values are the best ones, or that any output means something. *See also:* Computational Substrate; Seven Design Criteria.

**Mode Control Toggles** (*Toolkit, Stage 1*). The first toggle group: Alien Mode (default Off), Secondary Synthesis Audit (On), Show Full Density Metrics (On) and Density Probe (On, Base). Together they set the run’s perspective and how much ledger and density detail the output shows. *See also:* Toggle groups; All Mode.

**Multiplicity Spectrum** (*Framework P5; Unified Formal Model §7*). The range over which secondary expressions appear as transduced multiples: solitary (isolates), finite (clusters; the default, giving pragmatic yields) or infinite (spreads; optional). It provisions secondary expressions without forcing every variance into secondary form. *See also:* P5: Plurality of Expressions; Secondary expression; Meta-Loop.

## N

**No-input halt** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Skeleton*). When no expression is given there is nothing to score, and ρ is undefined (N = 0), so the run prints only a short P1 Door 2 (generative uncertainty or silence) halt note. A given expression whose secondaries all fall at or below τ is not this case; it has the full template with the Raw/Regressed yield. *See also:* All-isolated edge case; P1 Door 1 and Door 2.

**Non-Dual** (*Toolkit, Uncertainty toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that boosts mid-paradox tolerance and input variance; it switches on automatically for “void” or “paradox” keywords. *See also:* Emptiness-First (Non-Dual Priority); All Mode.

**Non-relational gaps** (*Framework P3; Toolkit P3*). The observable points at which secondary synthesis fails to cohere or point back to the primary expression; they register only inside an expression’s analysis, never as positive entities or extra nodes. They create uncertainty because they break or refuse the secondary synthesis loop, and a run names them in the Contrast & Non-Relational Gap Summary and the Uncertainty Table. *See also:* Gap; Synthesis Refusal; Non-relational Remainder.

**Non-relational Remainder** (*Framework P3*). The name for the limit of obtainability in any analysis. It names only the limit, not something left over; it is never produced by a run, is not Isolation Residual, and is not a third peer mode. Naming it is itself a relational act. *See also:* Gap; Isolation Residual (ρ); Obtainability.

**Normalized Bayesian posterior P_norm(x | V)** (*Unified Formal Model §3*). Standard Bayesian updating under a chosen prior (default uniform), followed by L1 renormalization so that the distribution sums to 1. It is the second factor of the hybrid function and supplies the graded evidential weight of each candidate relative to V. *See also:* Hybrid function H(x); Fuzzy membership function μ(x).

## O

**Obtainability** (*Framework P1; Toolkit, Spectra*). To obtain, as the chapters use it, is for an expression to stabilize as a provisional unit through secondary synthesis; it is not a claim about what exists apart from analysis (P1). Obtainability is how far something can be registered within an analysis, read through the hybrid function and the obtainability spectrum. If obtainability falls below threshold, the analysis registers uncertainty or silence, and the Non-relational Remainder marks its limit. *See also:* Dilemma gate; Spectra; Non-relational Remainder.

**Open Field** (*Framework P1*). The widest synthesis space available once analysis of an expression has begun. *See also:* P1: The Open Field.

**Other-pointing** (*Framework P3; Unified Formal Model §6*). The mode in which an expression’s pointing-aspect stabilizes toward distinct repeatable entities or other expressions. P(Other-pointing) is one of the two modes of the Inferred Relator Mode Distribution. *See also:* Self-pointing; Two primary modes.

**Output Format** (*Toolkit, Specialized Probes*). A parameter, default Readable, that states what the final output should look like or contain. *See also:* Light Output.

## P

**P1: The Open Field** (*Framework P1; Toolkit P1*). If analysis begins on an expression that has already obtained, the widest synthesis space within that analysis is the Open Field, where secondary expressions may be abducted and aligned or their absence or refusal registered; if no expression obtains, the chain halts at unobtainable silence. The Open Field is not a prior ontological field but the space that opens when analysis begins, and P1 does not say what exists. *See also:* Dilemma gate; P1 Door 1 and Door 2; Staging Axiom.

**P1 Door 1 and Door 2** (*Framework P1; Toolkit*). The two outcomes of the dilemma gate: Door 1 proceeds with relational contrasts available for further distinctions; Door 2 registers generative uncertainty or silence when obtainability falls below threshold. A run with no expression given prints only a P1 Door 2 halt note. *See also:* Dilemma gate; P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2; No-input halt.

**P2: Contrasting Parts in the Field** (*Framework P2; Toolkit P2*). If analysis proceeds, contrasting parts may emerge as the differentiations that appear when secondary expressions are brought into relation, and they provision distinctions for analysis of the pointing-aspect. Uniform sameness leaves the pointing-aspect minimally differentiated; elements that will not cohere remain as uncertainties. *See also:* Contrasting parts.

**P3: Relational Construction of Contrasting Entities** (*Framework P3; Toolkit P3*). Contrasting parts undergo relational construction: bifurcation into connectable paths or unconnectable gaps, co-emergence with repeatability, and decomposition into relateds and relators operating in self-pointing or other-pointing mode. The Non-relational Remainder marks the limit of obtainability and Isolation Residual rises when pointing is refused; neither is a third peer mode. *See also:* Relational construction; Relational Density Spectrum; Non-relational Remainder.

**P4: Layering in Expressions** (*Framework P4; Toolkit P4 (Hierarchy Gate)*). Relators and relateds that obtain on repeatable relational entities organize into layered expressions (subatomic singles, atomic sequences or complex nests), which analysis can treat as standing on their own at ~1 certainty. Unorganizable elements regress to undecomposable uncertainties or unrepeatable uniques. *See also:* Layer Spectrum (LayerSpectrum); Atomic expression.

**P5: Plurality of Expressions** (*Framework P5; Toolkit P5 (Scale Gate)*). Layered expressions appear as a current primary expression co-existing with provisional others: secondary expressions as transduced multiples, whether solitary, finite or infinite. That co-existence enables relational calibration without presuming independence or substrates. *See also:* Current primary expression; Multiplicity Spectrum.

**P6: Comparability and Measurement of Expressions** (*Framework P6; Toolkit P6 (Secondary Transduction Gate)*). With plurality, comparability arises as a spectrum of alignability and transduction (sparse, partial or dense) that provisions distances and divergences as utilities; unalignable elements regress to uncertainties with reciprocal boosts. P6 also carries the default interpretive restraint. *See also:* Alignability (AlignSpectrum); Default interpretive restraint; Transduction.

**P7: Evaluation and Yield** (*Framework P7; Toolkit P7 (Adjudication Gate)*). The analysis aggregates persistent unmappables as uncertainties, maximizes relational secondary expressions, and adjudicates the dialectical sum into a provisional yield: balanced, tensive, tempered or raw. The yield spectrum itself encodes the self-reflexive evaluation; no separate gate asks whether the whole chain holds. *See also:* Yield Spectrum; Yield Type.

**P8: Perception and Orientation** (*Framework P8 (Return of Responsibility); Toolkit P8 (Re-Expression and Open Integration Gate)*). Once yields obtain, perception registers the completion of the current analysis and returns responsibility for the next orientation to the user: continued refinement, modular incorporation, suspension, or setting the distinctions aside, all within the same Open Field. The framework claims no ongoing jurisdiction once the distinctions are not in use, and P8 is the renewal mechanism under which anything in the schema may be re-expressed. *See also:* P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2; P8 Gate; Functional Role Shift.

**P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2** (*Framework P8; Toolkit, P8 Gate*). The two outcomes at P8. P8 Door 1 is provisional acceptance: the current yield stands provisionally as complete. P8 Door 2 is open re-expression, including discard (refinement, addition, deletion, restructuring, modular incorporation or outright discard, optionally looping back to any prior starting point); the names tell these apart from the two Doors of P1. *See also:* P8: Perception and Orientation; P8 Gate; Retained Openness.

**P8 Gate** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template; Critiques*). The output slot that closes a run with the lock “Yield ≠ meaning; P8 re-expression still open” and entries for P8 Door 1 (provisional acceptance) and P8 Door 2 (open re-expression). In the Critiques it is where a look can be put down and where open questions are left. *See also:* P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2; Locks.

**Peircean Abduction Heuristic** (*Toolkit, Equity and Secondary Sourcing toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that ranks candidate secondaries by explanatory coherence before Dirichlet sampling; it is intended to improve secondary quality and reduce noise. *See also:* Secondary Abduction; Dirichlet priors.

**Pentalemma Variant** (*Toolkit, Output toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that adds a spectrum across affirm, negate, both and neither for fluid expressions, in place of the standard tetralemma or binary; it is optional from P1 and switches on automatically for non-binary flows. *See also:* Tetralemma / pentalemma mapping.

**Per-Item Uncertainty** (*Toolkit, Output toggles*). A toggle, default Off, that shows per-certainty and per-uncertainty probability variants in Stage 3 instead of only the global uncertainty probability. *See also:* uncertainty_prob_global.

**Perspective / Secondary Lens** (*Toolkit, Specialized Probes*). A parameter, default auto, that names which secondary expressions, lenses or sources to prioritize. *See also:* Secondary Abduction.

**Phenomenal Probe** (*Toolkit, Specialized Probes*). A toggle, “Off (auto for phenomenon)”, that widens sigma and treats non-linguistic inputs as raw phenomenal flows (+0.05–0.12 sigma, +0.15 uncertainties, granular sensations). *See also:* Data Type; Sigma (σ); Transduction.

**Pointing / pointing-aspect** (*Framework, A Note on Primitives*). The directed, representational or reflexive aspect of an expression: its capacity to stabilize toward itself (self-pointing) or toward distinct repeatable entities (other-pointing). Pointing is not the primitive, and the framework does not address the ultimate status of what the pointing-aspect refers to. *See also:* Expression; Primitive; Two primary modes.

**Pointing refused** (*Unified Formal Model §6.1; Toolkit*). The label for a component at or below τ: its directedness is undefined, it contributes no scores, and it appears under the Synthesis Refusal tag. No positive directedness is attributed to Isolation Residual. *See also:* Synthesis Refusal; Directedness differential (δ).

**Primary-as-Anchor Priority** (*Computational Substrate §3 (iii) and §4.1; Toolkit, Secondary Abduction*). The primary is the anchor: it is treated as a related at ~1 certainty, it stays dynamic while the secondaries are held static for the run (P5 anchor priority), and Alignment to Primary carries the largest SCS weight. *See also:* Alignment to Primary; Seven Design Criteria.

**Primary expression** (*Framework; Toolkit*). The expression under analysis; in a run, the input. It is treated primarily as a related whose pointing-aspect is examined, while secondary expressions act as relators, and the Toolkit does not construct it. *See also:* Current primary expression; Secondary expression.

**Primitive** (*Framework, A Note on Primitives; Critiques 3*). The framework’s starting point is expression, understood as a provisional stabilization of secondary synthesis; pointing is its directed aspect, not the primitive. Alternatives (pure presence, pure event or process, pure void or power, pure difference) were considered and set aside, and the choice is acknowledged as a trade-off. *See also:* Expression; Pointing / pointing-aspect; Bootstrap / origination asymmetry.

**Proto-claims and secondary claims** (*Toolkit, Input Scan and Phase 2*). Proto-claims are abducted from input chunks and for each secondary; the ledger is capped to the top seven by alignment. The claims required for the surviving secondary truths are listed with their stability in the Required Claims ledger. *See also:* Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger; Claim Coherence; Secondary Claim Spectrum.

## R

**Raw/Regressed Yield** (*Framework P7; Toolkit, Phase 6*). The yield type for an open pause or inevaluable state. It is forced when every component is isolated, the one case in which the Yield Type label is not the runner’s judgment. *See also:* Yield Type; All-isolated edge case.

**Reciprocal boost** (*Framework P6–P7; Unified Formal Model §1*). Unalignable, unadjudicable or below-threshold elements regress to uncertainties “with reciprocal boosts”: downstream uncertainty metrics rise where appropriate to keep the accounting consistent. All Mode supplies reciprocal uncertainty, and the Uncertainty Probe lists reciprocal boosts as its effect. *See also:* Uncertainty Probe; Uncertainty boost.

**Reflexive Tension** (*Toolkit, Phase 5 and Supporting Diagnostics*). Reflexive Tension is reported with avg_doubt (the mean doubt score of non-isolated components) immediately beneath the Tempered Certainty Ledger, and isolated components contribute to neither. It is a named readout with no formula in this book. *See also:* Certainty Temper Index; Tempered Certainty Ledger.

**Related** (*Framework P3*). A functional role: a passive anchor in relational construction. During analysis the primary expression is treated primarily as a related, and the same entity may be a related at one scale and a relator at another. *See also:* Relator; Functional Role Shift.

**Relational construction** (*Framework P3*). The decomposition, visible in analysis, of contrasting parts into connectable paths and unconnectable gaps, relateds and relators. Its distinctions (connectability, repeatability and relational mode) are revealed in analysis, not installed as prior features. *See also:* P3: Relational Construction of Contrasting Entities; Bifurcation; Relational Density Spectrum.

**Relational Density Spectrum** (*Framework P3–P4; Unified Formal Model §6*). The spectrum through which relators and relateds are revealed, made up of the connectability gradient, the repeatability spectrum and directedness (the two primary modes). The Density Probe partition of these dimensions is the default feature space for soft distributions. *See also:* Connectability gradient; Repeatability Spectrum; Two primary modes.

**Relational Index** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). A slot in Supporting Diagnostics. It is a named readout with no formula in this book. *See also:* Supporting Diagnostics.

**Relator** (*Framework P3*). A functional role: an active connector operating in self-pointing or other-pointing mode. In a run, the secondary expressions function as relators. *See also:* Related; Two primary modes.

**Repeatability Spectrum** (*Framework P3; Critiques 3*). How far connectable paths repeat; repeatability co-emerges with connectable paths in relational construction and is one dimension of the relational density spectrum. *See also:* Connectability gradient; Relational Density Spectrum; Resolution / Granularity.

**Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger** (*Toolkit, Phase 5*). Also called the Supporting Claims Ledger: the mandatory table of claims required for the secondary truths that survived hard isolation, with columns Starting Point / Claim, Stab (spectrum stability), UProb (associated uncertainty probability), Shaky (yes when Stab &lt; 0.50) and Note. Secondary Claim Temper, the harmonic mean of the Stab values, is reported in it and in Final Metrics. *See also:* Secondary Claim Temper (SCT); Shaky flag.

**Residual pole** (*Toolkit, Yields; Metrics Checks §6*). The uncertainty side of the tensive ratio. Isolation Residual is not the whole of it: uncertainties, generative pauses and Retained Openness stay outside ρ, and the Non-relational Remainder names only the limit and is never produced by a run. *See also:* Isolation Residual (ρ); Retained Openness; Generative pause.

**Residual-side diagnostics** (*Toolkit, Core Rules; Unified Formal Model §8*). Isolation Residual and post-isolation uncertainty, reported as commensurate with the Secondary Coherence Score. SCS is read together with ρ, because isolating weak components can raise SCS (Metrics Checks §10). *See also:* Isolation Residual (ρ); uncertainty_prob_global; Secondary Coherence Score (SCS).

**Resolution / Granularity** (*Toolkit, Specialized Probes*). A parameter, default Medium: Low clumps for stronger repeatability; High maximizes differences. *See also:* Repeatability Spectrum.

**Resonance** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). 1 − divergence, with ~0.85 read as aligned. *See also:* Stability; Uncertainty Resonance.

**Retained Openness** (*Toolkit, Yields*). What a run leaves open by design, for example a P8 door still open. It stays outside ρ. *See also:* Residual pole; P8 Door 1 and P8 Door 2.

**Run Metadata** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template*). The second slot of a run’s output: secondaries abducted, isolated components, ρ, post-isolation uncertainty_prob_global_avg, and input variance / sigma. *See also:* Header; Isolation Residual (ρ).

**Runner** (*Toolkit, Metrics and Data Type*). The Toolkit’s word for what carries out a run. Component values are the runner’s estimates, and what can be checked is the arithmetic from them; the runner decides how an input is read unless the user specifies, and apart from the forced Raw/Regressed yield the Yield Type label is the runner’s judgment. *See also:* Data Type; Yield Type; User, reader, you.

## S

**Score** (*Framework, A Note on Words; Toolkit, Overview*). The quantitative state of a look, not truth or meaning taken whole. Component values are the runner’s estimates; what can be checked is the arithmetic from them. *See also:* Look; Meaning.

**Secondary Abduction** (*Toolkit, Stage 1 and Phase 2*). Selecting and populating the secondary expressions for a run within P1’s Open Field, using Dirichlet priors for equity-balanced sampling (N = 15, tunable 12–20). The selected secondaries are anchored as static, repeatable references (dictionaries, encyclopedias, pattern databases, cultural priors, or transduced phenomenal descriptors) for the duration of the run. *See also:* Dirichlet priors; Peircean Abduction Heuristic; Secondary expression.

**Secondary Claim Spectrum** (*Toolkit, Spectra*). A spectrum in [0, 1]: high values register stable requirements for secondary truth, mid values tensive or shaky conditions, and low values unstable or odd elements. *See also:* Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger; Secondary Claim Temper (SCT).

**Secondary Claim Temper (SCT)** (*Toolkit, Phase 5 and Supporting Diagnostics; Unified Formal Model §8*). The harmonic mean of the per-secondary claim-stability (Stab) values on survivors, clamped to [0.3, 0.7]; a value below 0.5 reads as a shaky or odd flag. A run lists the Stab values, shows the mean and the clamp, and then enters SCT in the Claim-Tempered yield. *See also:* Claim-Tempered Yield; Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger; Shaky flag.

**Secondary Coherence Score (SCS)** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Toolkit, Metrics; Computational Substrate §4.1*). A major diagnostic of secondary synthesis strength among survivors: SCS = 0.40 × mean(Alignment to Primary) + 0.35 × mean(Inter-Secondary Coherence) + 0.25 × mean(Traction Provided), clamped to [0, 1], over components with traction > τ; if all are isolated it shows “N/A (all components isolated)”. It is not the definition of meaning and not the single central measure. Its target ranges, 0.52–0.78 (balanced/strong), 0.25 to below 0.52 (low-traction) and above 0.78 (very strong), are working conventions. *See also:* Alignment to Primary; Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC); Traction Provided; Residual-side diagnostics.

**Secondary expression** (*Framework P5; Toolkit, Overview*). A provisional other expression, a transduced multiple, that co-exists with the primary and functions as a relator in the analysis. Secondaries are the engine of a run: their meshing and pointing-back determine the standing of a primary already here, and they are held static for the run. *See also:* Secondary Abduction; Secondary synthesis; Survivors.

**Secondary Expression Equity** (*Toolkit, Equity and Secondary Sourcing toggles; Critiques 16*). A toggle, default On, that audits diversity and balances domains, including voids, with the Secondary Source family and Claim-Balanced as sub-modes. It is intended to reduce sampling imbalance; Critiques 16 asks whether that equity may be performative. *See also:* Dirichlet priors; Equity Sampler; Directional Bleed.

**Secondary synthesis** (*Framework, Introduction*). The coming together of secondary expressions whose coherent meshing, or refusal to cohere, determines a primary expression’s standing; it is the operative engine of the system, and an expression is a provisional stabilization of it. Where it is absent or refused, uncertainty arises because there is no pointing back. *See also:* Expression; Synthesis Refusal.

**Secondary Synthesis Audit** (*Toolkit, Mode Control Toggles*). A toggle, default On, that promotes the Secondary Synthesis Ledger and Traction Flow Diagram to the top as the visual diagnostic section, with richer detail. *See also:* Secondary Synthesis Ledger; Traction Flow Diagram.

**Secondary Synthesis Ledger** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template; Framework P6*). The table giving each secondary expression’s mode, alignment (Align), Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC) and traction: the per-secondary values behind the Secondary Coherence Score. *See also:* Secondary Coherence Score (SCS); Secondary Synthesis Audit.

**Secondary Transduction Gate** (*Framework P6; Toolkit P6*). The P6 gate, at which comparability arises as a spectrum of alignability and transduction. *See also:* Alignability (AlignSpectrum); Transduction; Gates.

**Self-pointing** (*Framework P3; Unified Formal Model §6*). The mode in which an expression’s pointing-aspect stabilizes toward itself (reflexive identity). P(Self-pointing) is one of the two modes of the Inferred Relator Mode Distribution. *See also:* Other-pointing; Two primary modes.

**Seven Design Criteria** (*Computational Substrate §3*). The jointly necessary criteria every parameter is meant to satisfy: (i) Hard Isolation Preservation; (ii) Two-Mode Restriction & Isolation Residual; (iii) Primary-as-Anchor Priority; (iv) Operational Restraint Toward Non-Relational Gaps; (v) Empirical Stability (a bounded 10–20% parameter change should produce no violation of (i)–(iv) and no runaway divergence, apart from steps where a component crosses τ or the survivor count falls to one or none); (vi) Revisability Under P8; (vii) Derivability from General Apparatus. *See also:* Computational Substrate; Metrics Checks; Governance Protocol.

**Shaky flag** (*Toolkit, Phase 5*). A yes/no flag in the Required Claims ledger, triggered when a claim’s Stab is below 0.50. *See also:* Secondary Claim Temper (SCT); Required Claims for Secondary Truth Ledger.

**Show Full Density Metrics** (*Toolkit, Mode Control Toggles*). A toggle, default On, that unlocks the complete Supporting Diagnostics in Final Metrics, a fuller Metric Evolution Table and expanded table and ledger detail; Off gives lean, scannable output. *See also:* Supporting Diagnostics; Mode Control Toggles.

**Sigma (σ)** (*Toolkit, Stage 1*). A run parameter derived in Stage 1 together with input variance and reported with it in Run Metadata. It widens for porous or high-tension inputs: to 0.12 for inputs read as phenomenal or non-linguistic, by +0.05–0.12 under Phenomenal Probe and +0.15 under Alien Mode, with higher Chaos Scale values, and to its maximum at the Staging Axiom’s first act. *See also:* Input variance; Data Type; Chaos Scale.

**Soft distribution** (*Computational Substrate §4.1.1; Toolkit, Metrics*). The normalized distribution Pᵢ of each surviving secondary over a shared feature space F, formed from the weights H(f | ·) for f ∈ F and normalized (L1 preferred). It feeds Alignment to Primary, Inter-Secondary Coherence, the SCS and δ; the primary default for F is the relational-density dimensions of P3 (the Density Probe partition). *See also:* Alignment to Primary; Inter-Secondary Coherence (ISC); Density Probe.

**Spectra** (*Toolkit, Spectra; Unified Formal Model §1*). The continuous quantities on [0, 1] that a run computes on survivors: obtainability, contrast, repeatability, layering, multiplicity, alignment and yield, plus the Secondary Claim Spectrum. All inherit from the hybrid function, and tetralemma mapping supplies gradual shadings. *See also:* Hybrid function H(x); Obtainability; Yield Spectrum.

**Stability** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). 1 − average delta, with ~0.8 read as solid. *See also:* Resonance; Uncertainty Stability.

**Stages and Phases** (*Toolkit, Operational Workflow*). A run has three stages: Stage 1 (Input and Customization), Stage 2 (Dialectical and Secondary Expression Analysis) and Stage 3 (Reflection and Yield). Stage 2 runs six phases (Ph 1 Foundations, Ph 2 Secondaries, Ph 3 Certainties, Ph 4 Uncertainties, Ph 5 Reflexive Temper, Ph 6 Aggregation), which are the columns of the Metric Evolution Table. *See also:* Metric Evolution Table; Canonical Output Template.

**Staging Axiom** (*Unified Formal Model, opening and §10; Toolkit P1*). The first operational act: given a candidate primary E0, abduct a provisional secondary set S0 by applying H under a default uniform prior and maximal σ. If every traction is ≤ τ, the yield is immediately Raw/Regressed with full isolation and the chain halts (Synthesis Refusal at the system’s own conditions of possibility); otherwise S0 becomes the initial secondary layer. A given expression that isolates fully in this way still prints the full template with the Raw/Regressed yield (All-isolated edge case); only a run with no expression given prints the halt note alone (No-input halt). *See also:* Bootstrap / origination asymmetry; All-isolated edge case; No-input halt.

**Starting points** (*Toolkit*). The Toolkit’s name for the eight presumptions P1–P8. *See also:* Eight presumptions (P1–P8).

**Subatomic expression** (*Framework P4*). A lone related functioning strictly as a passive anchor; the layer of singles. *See also:* Atomic expression; Complex expression.

**Supporting Diagnostics** (*Toolkit, Metrics and Canonical Output Template*). The readouts shown when Show Full Density Metrics is On: Meaning Tension (a dark slot), Resonance, Stability, Uncertainty Resonance, Uncertainty Stability, Avg Density, Layer Spectrum Avg, Certainty Temper Index, Reflexive Tension, Relational Index, Secondary Claim Temper, Claim Coherence and mean δ. Of these, δ is a pure reading instrument, as is Tsallis density, with no effect on isolation, core yields or the mode distribution. The Toolkit and Unified Formal Model §6.6 call the block of final figures Final Metrics; it carries the isolation count, ρ, post-isolation uncertainty, SCT and these readouts. *See also:* Show Full Density Metrics; Directedness differential (δ); Tsallis density (ϕ).

**Survivors** (*Framework P6; Toolkit*). The secondary expressions left after hard isolation, those with traction > τ. Every average and distribution is computed on survivors only; isolated components enter only through ρ. With a single survivor, Inter-Secondary Coherence is 1 by definition. *See also:* Hard Isolation Rule; Secondary Coherence Score (SCS).

**Synthesis Refusal** (*Framework P3; Toolkit; Unified Formal Model §6*). The measurable limit of any secondary-synthesis audit: where secondary synthesis is absent or refused there is no pointing back, and unlike the Non-relational Remainder it is marked in a run and measured through ρ. A component at or below τ carries the full label “Synthesis Refusal active — traction ≤ τ — pointing refused (excluded from aggregation and distributions)” on first occurrence in each major section. Thereafter it may be shortened to “Synthesis Refusal active — traction ≤ τ — pointing refused”, “Synthesis Refusal active”, “Synthesis Refusal active — no pointing back” or “Synthesis refused — no pointing back”. *See also:* Hard Isolation Rule; Pointing refused; Isolation Residual (ρ).

## T

**Tau (τ)** (*Unified Formal Model §1 and §6; Computational Substrate §4.2*). The dilemma-gate and hard-isolation threshold: τ = 0.001 by default, adjustable under P8 within [0.0001, 0.01]. A component with traction ≤ τ is isolated, and outputs step where a move in τ crosses a component’s traction (Metrics Checks §12). *See also:* Dilemma gate; Hard Isolation Rule.

**Tempered Certainty Ledger** (*Toolkit, Phase 5*). The mandatory table giving one row to every non-isolated secondary in the Certainty Table, with columns Secondary Expression, Unaddressed Starting Point, Tetralemma, Tempered Geo/Harm, C/U Ratio (certainty-to-uncertainty ratio), Temper Index and Note. avg_doubt and Reflexive Tension appear beneath it and never replace the rows. *See also:* Unaddressed Starting Point; Certainty Temper Index; Reflexive Tension.

**Tempered Yield** (*Framework P7*). The yield type with low certainty dominance. *See also:* Yield Type.

**Temper-Probe** (*Toolkit, Certainty Tempering toggles*). A toggle, default On, that previews temper aggressiveness (Soft sigmoid or Hard binary), automatically for paradoxes; it is active in Phase 4 and tempers doubts for fairness. *See also:* Certainty Temper Probe.

**Tension of Certainty** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Metrics Checks §6*). The Formal Model’s name for the Tensive yield, defined identically to it. It is a yield, not a meaning score and not a second meaning number. *See also:* Tensive.

**Tensive** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Toolkit, Yields*). One formal operationalization of the Framework’s tensive ratio, provisional under P8: Tensive = √(SCS × (1 − ρ)), defined when at least one survivor exists. At a given SCS it falls as ρ rises, and it is not the definition of meaning. *See also:* Tension of Certainty; Claim-Tempered Yield; Tensive Yield.

**Tensive Yield** (*Framework P7*). The yield type for a mid certainty–uncertainty mix. In the Formal Model and the Toolkit, Tensive is also a number; those are not the same object. *See also:* Tensive; Yield Type.

**Tetralemma / pentalemma mapping** (*Unified Formal Model §5; Framework P1*). Graded mappings on [0, 1], tetralemma (affirm, negate, both, neither) or the optional pentalemma, used as analytical tools when an expression shows paradox, fluidity or mid-spectrum tension. They never override hard isolation or the two-mode structure. *See also:* Pentalemma Variant; Tempered Certainty Ledger.

**Toggle groups** (*Toolkit, Stage 1*). The six toggle tables: Mode Control; Equity and Secondary Sourcing; Uncertainty and Non-Relational Handling; Certainty Tempering and Reflexive Analysis; Iteration, Output, and Presentation Control; and Specialized Probes and Technical Parameters. Toggles the user has not set may be derived from the input, and every setting stays provisional under P7 and P8. *See also:* All Mode; Mode Control Toggles; Toggles Active.

**Toggles Active** (*Toolkit, Header*). The Header line listing the toggles in force for a run, including any switched on automatically by an input-triggered rule. *See also:* All Mode; Header.

**Toolkit, The** (*Volume Two*). The operational procedure that applies the eight starting points to any primary expression through the same ontologically permissive workflow, deriving connectable certainties from provisional secondaries and identifying where secondary synthesis fails or is refused. Outputs vary from run to run, so three or more runs of the same expression give a sense of the range; the yield it operationalizes is not meaning. *See also:* Canonical Output Template; Stages and Phases.

**Traction** (*Toolkit; Unified Formal Model §6*). How far a component points back in secondary synthesis, scored at the secondary level by the hybrid value Hᵢ and compared with τ at the dilemma gate before isolation. Traction at or below τ (no pointing back at all) triggers hard isolation. *See also:* Traction Provided; Hard Isolation Rule; Tau (τ).

**Traction Flow Diagram** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template; Unified Formal Model §6.6*). A required slot, preferred default Style A: directed ASCII from E0 through self-pointing and other-pointing survivors, with ρ parked outside the two-mode flow, to Align / ISC / Tr, SCS and Tensive. Isolated components are visually distinguished and labeled “Synthesis Refusal active”; the layout may vary under P8 if the labels and locks hold. *See also:* Secondary Synthesis Ledger; Locks.

**Traction Provided** (*Unified Formal Model §8; Toolkit, Metrics*). The identity specialization tractionᵢ ≡ Hᵢ (licensed default, revisable under P8), evaluated before isolation. Only survivors enter its mean, which carries weight 0.25 in the Secondary Coherence Score. *See also:* Traction; Secondary Coherence Score (SCS).

**Transduction** (*Framework P6 (On the Epistemic Status and Limits of Transduction); Toolkit, Input Scan*). The conversion of raw activation patterns into relational secondary expressions that can be abducted, aligned and measured. It is fallible, selective and reductive, and the transduced representation is a provisional expression about an experience, not the experience itself. The response to transduction error is more secondary expressions, along with wider sigma, reciprocal uncertainty for porous inputs, and explicit refusal labels. *See also:* Input variance; Data Type; Alignability (AlignSpectrum).

**Truth** (*Framework, Introduction*). Truth appears, inside a look, as guessed probabilistic relations among connectable elements. *See also:* Meaning; Certainties.

**Tsallis density (ϕ)** (*Computational Substrate §4.6; Metrics Checks §13*). A supporting reading reported beside Avg Density, on a comparable scale and the same Phase-1 distribution: S_q = (1 − Σᵢ pᵢ^q)/(q − 1), q ≠ 1, and ϕ = S_q / S_q^max(k, q) with S_q^max(k, q) = (1 − k^(1−q))/(q − 1), where k is the support size and the default q = 0.9. It never alters isolation and never enters the mode distribution or SCS; it is undefined for a support of one item. *See also:* Avg Density; Supporting Diagnostics.

**Two primary modes** (*Framework; Unified Formal Model §6*). Self-pointing (reflexive identity) and other-pointing (distinct repeatable references), the only modes the Inferred Relator Mode Distribution recognizes. Isolation Residual and the Non-relational Remainder are not third modes. *See also:* Self-pointing; Other-pointing; Inferred Relator Mode Distribution.

## U

**Unaddressed Starting Point** (*Toolkit, Phase 5*). The column of the Tempered Certainty Ledger naming the P1–P8 label that triggered a doubt. *See also:* Tempered Certainty Ledger; Eight presumptions (P1–P8).

**Uncertainties** (*Framework P1; Toolkit, Phase 4*). Unmappable variants, persistent negations, or inputs whose obtainability stays below threshold, registered as observed limits of the current secondary synthesis. Phase 4 builds uncertainties from high unconnectable non-relational gaps (minimum 5, cap 10). *See also:* Certainties; Uncertainty Table; Meaning.

**Uncertainty boost** (*Unified Formal Model §6.4; Computational Substrate §4.4; Metrics Checks §4*). The one controlled step by which isolation density enters reported uncertainty: uncertainty_prob_global ← clamp(uncertainty_prob_global + 0.05 × ρ, 0.25, 0.9), where the floor reflects that every look stays provisional and the ceiling that a run begins only once something has come forward (both bounds are working defaults). A look in which nothing holds is handled by hard isolation and P1 Door 2, not by this figure. At the ceiling the clamp can lower a figure, so the boost is not always an increase. *See also:* uncertainty_prob_global; Isolation Residual (ρ); Reciprocal boost.

**Uncertainty Probe** (*Toolkit, Uncertainty toggles*). A toggle, default On (unconditionally, with the Echo sub-mode, which fades older or less relevant secondaries, and the Feedback-Adaptive sub-mode, which retro-tempers Phase 1–2 metrics after later phases, active), that abducts and boosts non-relational gaps; its sub-modes are Echo, Feedback-Adaptive, Inverse and Phase-Locked. When Off, it activates only above 0.45 uncertainty; its effect is reciprocal boosts. *See also:* Reciprocal boost; Non-relational gaps.

**uncertainty_prob_global** (*Toolkit, Stage 1 and Phase 6; Unified Formal Model §6.4*). The run’s global uncertainty probability, raised early for low input variance or for short or vague inputs and adjusted after isolation by the uncertainty boost. Its post-isolation average (uncertainty_prob_global_avg) is reported in Run Metadata and the Metric Evolution Table. *See also:* Uncertainty boost; Per-Item Uncertainty; Residual-side diagnostics.

**Uncertainty Resonance** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). 1 − inter_void, with ~0.65 read as tensive. *See also:* Resonance; Uncertainty Stability.

**Uncertainty Stability** (*Toolkit, Supporting Diagnostics*). 1 − std of deltas plus a chaos adjustment, with ~0.65 read as tensive. *See also:* Stability; Chaos Scale.

**Uncertainty Table** (*Toolkit, Canonical Output Template; Unified Formal Model §6.6*). The table (ID, Type, Item, Prob / Tension, Notes) of a run’s uncertainties, including every isolated component with its Synthesis Refusal label. Isolated components move only here and are excluded from the Certainty Table and the Tempered Certainty Ledger. *See also:* Uncertainties; Synthesis Refusal.

**uncertainty_yield_geo_avg** (*Toolkit, Phase 4*). A Phase 4 yield, reported with uncertainty_res, uncertainty_stab and uncertainty_prob_global_avg. *See also:* Uncertainties; Certainty yield geo avg.

**Unified Formal Model** (*Volume Two*). The general mathematical machinery (hybrid function, fuzzy membership families, normalized Bayesian posteriors, dilemma gate, tetralemma mapping, hard isolation rule and inheritance rules) by which the Toolkit’s metrics, phases and ledgers are licensed as operational specializations. It is offered as a representation, not as a foundation or higher authority, and is provisional under P8. *See also:* Computational Substrate; Hybrid function H(x); Staging Axiom.

**User, reader, you** (*Framework, A Note on Words*). Whatever takes up the next expression; each is itself an expression, not a performer standing outside the analysis. *See also:* P8: Perception and Orientation.

## V

**Volume One** (*Critiques 15*). The plain account of the idea without the machinery: one line at the top of a page, a short list, four marks, and a rim; in its own words, “A pen is enough. A machine is not required.” Its working words (leftover, unlisted, rim) and its cup page are defined there, not here. *See also:* Look; Light Output.

## Y

**Yield Spectrum** (*Framework P7; Toolkit, Yields*). The spectrum of provisional outputs that encodes the self-reflexive evaluation of P7: four types (Balanced, Tensive, Tempered, Raw/Regressed) and, in a run, the Sum, Geo, Harmonic, Tensive and Claim-Tempered variants computed on survivors. Values above 0.5 indicate relational utility, a working convention that no check ties to use. *See also:* Yield Type; Tensive; Claim-Tempered Yield.

**Yield Type** (*Framework P7; Toolkit, Executive Yield*). The label for a run’s yield: Balanced, Tensive, Tempered or Raw/Regressed. Apart from the Raw/Regressed yield forced when every component is isolated, the label is the runner’s judgment; the numbers govern. *See also:* Yield Spectrum; Raw/Regressed Yield.
