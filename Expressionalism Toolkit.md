## **Overview**

The Expressionalism Toolkit actively applies the eight starting points of the framework to any primary expression. It treats pointing as the directed, representational, or reflexive aspect of an expression. A primary expression may be a linguistic statement, scientific claim, work of art, personal experience, memory, neuron firing, bird call, song, movie, political document, logical paradox, visual scene, pattern of marks in sand, image never previously encountered, or moment of silence.

The toolkit processes every input as an undifferentiated datum through the same ontologically permissive workflow. It derives connectable certainties from provisional secondary expressions and explicitly identifies locations where secondary synthesis fails or is refused. Meaning is measured as the tensive ratio between those certainties and the uncertainties that remain. Secondary expressions form the actual engine of the system: the toolkit constructs every primary expression from prior secondary expressions whose coherent meshing and pointing-back determine its standing. Where secondary synthesis is absent or refused, uncertainty arises because there is no pointing back — this is Synthesis Refusal, the measurable limit of any secondary-synthesis audit.

P1 serves as the ontologically open permissive synthesis field within which the toolkit abducts and aligns secondary expressions prior to reverse-engineering the secondary synthesis that constitutes the current primary expression and examining its pointing-aspect at P8. The toolkit accepts linguistic, phenomenal, visual, multimodal, or any other datum, maintaining full openness across domains while treating secondary expressions as static, reliable anchors for the duration of each run. All outputs remain provisional, fallible, and subject to the framework’s own grammar.

## **Core Rules at a Glance**

* Secondary Coherence Score is a major diagnostic of secondary synthesis strength among survivors (computed exclusively on non-isolated components). Residual-side diagnostics (Isolation Residual and post-isolation uncertainty) are commensurate.

## **Metrics**

### **Secondary Coherence Score (major diagnostic among survivors)**

A major diagnostic of secondary synthesis strength among survivors in standard runs (when Secondary Synthesis Audit \= ON or Show Full Density Metrics \= OFF — the recommended default). The toolkit uses it to quantify how coherently abducted secondary expressions align with one another and point back to the primary expression. It is not the definition of meaning.

**Formula**

Secondary Coherence Score \= 0.40 × mean(Alignment to Primary) \+ 0.35 × mean(Inter-Secondary Coherence) \+ 0.25 × mean(Traction Provided)

(clamped to \[0, 1\]).

All three means are computed exclusively over components with traction \> τ after hard isolation has been applied. The Inter-Coherence values appearing in the Certainty Table and Secondary Synthesis Ledger are exactly the per-secondary quantities whose mean is the Inter-Secondary Coherence term above.

The operational definition of the per-secondary Inter-Secondary Coherence scalar is given in the Unified Formal Model, §8 (Alignability and Yield Spectra), as the average pairwise similarity 1 − JSD of each non-isolated secondary’s soft distribution to the soft distributions of the other non-isolated secondaries (with the convention ISC1 \= 1 when only one non-isolated secondary remains). The Computational Substrate, §4.1.1, justifies this definition as a direct specialization of the existing soft-vector apparatus and records the exact duality that the mean of the per-secondary values equals 1 − JSDavg when N ≥ 2\. The printed Inter-Coherence column is identical to that quantity; its mean, together with the means of Alignment to Primary and Traction Provided, produces the Secondary Coherence Score according to the formula above. No undocumented post-processing intervenes.

### **Soft Distribution Construction (licensed minimal default, fully revisable under P8)**

Soft distributions Pi that feed Alignment-to-Primary, Inter-Secondary Coherence (1 − JSD), Secondary Coherence Score, and the directedness differential are obtained as follows (definition in Unified Formal Model §8; Computational Substrate §4.1.1 justifies under the seven design criteria):

The shared feature space F is generated from secondary synthesis itself (primary licensed default: the relational-density dimensions of starting point P3 / Density Probe partition; licensed alternatives include Toolkit chunk spectra or other secondary-synthesis partitions).

For each secondary i, form unnormalized weights wf \= H(f | secondary\_i) for f ∈ F.

Hard isolation is applied first: any component with H ≤ τ or traction\_i ≤ τ is excluded before normalization.

Preferred default normalization on the surviving weights: L1 sum-normalize, Pi(f) \= wf / ∑f wf provided ∑f wf \> 0\. If the sum of surviving weights is exactly 0, the secondary falls under the existing N \= 0 / undefined conventions already used for SCS and JSD. Softmax, supremum mapping, and CDF mapping remain licensed normalization options under P8.

Explicit order of operations: hybrid evaluation → traction evaluation → hard isolation → soft-vector construction on survivors only. The primary is treated as related at ∼1 certainty (P5). Alignment-to-Primary, Inter-Secondary Coherence, SCS, and the directedness differential are then computed on these Pi.

## **Operational Workflow**

### **Stage 1: Input and Customization**

**Purpose**

This stage assumes acceptance of P1–P3 unless deep triage is explicitly selected. It customizes for domain and equity while avoiding early halts for most inputs. Three integrated parts operate: raw input handling (detection and proxying), optional toggles (user-customizable parameters for equity and domain emphases), and processing (derivation of input variance and sigma, relational bifurcation, uncertainty probability calculation, and generation of a hint that emphasizes provisionality and the P8 renewal loop). The toolkit abducts all secondary expressions within the ontologically open permissive synthesis field of P1 before assessment of the current primary expression (treated primarily as a related whose pointing-aspect is under examination) at P8. This process of selecting and populating secondary expressions is termed Secondary Abduction.

The formal apparatus and all derived Toolkit procedures are defined only for inputs already presented as primary expressions (per the Staging Axiom in P1: first operational act under uniform prior \+ maximal σ; total traction ≤ τ yields Raw/Regressed labeled Synthesis Refusal at the system’s own conditions of possibility).

**Input Scan**

The toolkit transduces the input into input variance. Text receives semantic-density-normalized variance via abductive partitioning into logical chunks using thematic coherence. Images yield pixel gradients and clusters. PDFs supply keyword density from snippets. URLs undergo simulated fetch and processing. Non-verbal or multimodal inputs are treated as chunked flows of gradient patterns or beats.

Chunks are formed, standard deviation is computed per chunk on fuzzy memberships drawn from P1–P4 spectra, and input variance is aggregated via harmonic mean clamped to \[0.03, 0.6\]. Phenomenal or non-linguistic inputs trigger auto-detection of data\_type \= ‘phenomenon’ with sigma widened to 0.12 for porous flows. Explicit transduction rules convert phenomena into descriptor chunks. Low input variance produces an early uncertainty\_prob\_global boost (+0.15 for sparse sensations). Proto-claims are abducted from chunks. All secondary expressions — linguistic, phenomenal, or transduced — are held static for the current analysis.

**Secondary Abduction**

Within P1’s ontologically open field, the toolkit performs Secondary Abduction by drawing candidate secondary expressions using Dirichlet priors for equity-balanced sampling across domains and traditions. The optional Peircean Abduction Heuristic ranks candidates first by explanatory coherence before sampling. Selected secondaries are anchored as static, repeatable references (dictionaries, encyclopedias, pattern databases, cultural priors, or transduced phenomenal descriptors) that remain fixed for the duration of the run, enabling reliable verification and reverse-engineering of the primary expression’s pointing-aspect. The primary expression remains dynamic while secondaries are held static for the run (P5 anchor priority).
